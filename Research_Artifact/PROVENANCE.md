# Source and measurement provenance

ReceiptCoordinator is an independent research distribution developed from the authors' prior configuration-management implementation. Copyright and the original PolyForm Noncommercial license are retained in LICENSE. This is a namespace and packaging release, not a claim that every supporting adapter or management utility was newly invented.

The manuscript's performance results are the frozen v17 measurements, not timings measured on this renamed binary. In distribution copies of recorded data, namespace and path strings are mapped for readability; numeric observations, outcomes, method identifiers and batch membership are unchanged. Historical SHA-256 fields remain historical and must not be compared with new source files. The original byte-exact material and source-to-release mapping remain in the development archive. The release manifest hashes this distribution. New checks are stored separately in validation/.

Public repository payloads and their licenses are copied without alteration. They are test data, not executed project workloads. IEEEtran retains its copyright and LPPL notices.
